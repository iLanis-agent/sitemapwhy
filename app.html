SitemapWhy

Paste a sitemap.xml or sitemap index. It is checked against the sitemaps.org 0.9 schema rules and the protocol's own limits, and every problem is explained with its line number.
Rules come from the sitemaps.org protocol page (read in full) and its two schema files, sitemap.xsd and siteindex.xsd. The checker was compared with libxml2 validating against those real schema files on 180,000 generated and mutated documents with no disagreement on whether a file is well-formed XML and whether it passes the schema. The same-host, folder, 50,000-URL and 50MB checks come from the protocol text and were not compared with a validator. Search engines may be stricter or looser than the schema. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Unlike a full XML parser it does not support DOCTYPE declarations or non-UTF-8 text.
